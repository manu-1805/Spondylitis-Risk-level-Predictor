Spondylitis

Student Project Risk Form

Spondylitis is an inflammatory condition that affects the spine and nearby joints. It can lead to pain, stiffness, and reduced flexibility if symptoms are ignored.

This web is a project of students of MNIT Jaipur. It gives a brief educational form based on posture, sleep, lifestyle, and pain-related questions.

Prepared by MNIT Jaipur students for educational use
Brief Introduction

About Spondylitis

Spondylitis usually affects the back and spine. Common warning signs include stiffness, posture-related pain, sleep discomfort, and long sitting hours.

About This Website

This website is kept brief and simple. It is a student project from MNIT Jaipur that collects short response data for awareness and study purposes.

Common Risk Signals
Sedentary Lifestyle Poor Posture Morning Stiffness Back or Heel Pain Low Physical Activity Disturbed Sleep
